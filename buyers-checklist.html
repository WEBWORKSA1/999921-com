✅ Your checklist

Before you buy

  1. Check today’s live price per gram for your purity.
  2. Know the hallmark: 9999, 999, 916 (22K), 875 (21K), 750 (18K).
  3. For bullion, compare premium over spot — under 3% for 1 kg/100 g bars, 4–8% for 1 oz coins is typical.
  4. Ask for an itemised invoice: weight × rate + making + tax.
  5. Making charges are negotiable — start at half the quoted rate.
  6. Avoid “wastage” fees on machine-made pieces.
  7. Buy recognised brands/refiners for easy resale.
  8. Check assay cards and serial numbers on bars.
  9. Prefer weights with 8/9, avoid 4 for gifts (see decoder).

After you buy

  1. Keep receipts and certificates together.
  2. Photograph pieces & hallmarks for insurance.
  3. Store bars in original packaging.
  4. Insure above your home-policy limit.

When you sell

  1. Weigh at home first (use a 0.01 g scale).
  2. Separate by karat — never sell mixed at the lowest rate.
  3. Get at least three offers — free here.
  4. Ask “what % of melt are you paying?” and compare with our calculator.
