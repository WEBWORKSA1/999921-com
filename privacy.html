Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What we collect

  • Information you submit in forms (name, email, phone, city, gold details, messages).
  • Preferences stored in your browser (currency, cookie choice, poll vote). These never leave your device.
  • Usage data collected by third-party services such as Google AdSense if you consent.

How we use it

  • To answer inquiries and deliver price alerts, newsletters and lead magnets you request.
  • For quote requests: to share your request with up to three matched partners, only when you tick the consent box.
  • To run contests and contact winners.

Form processing

Forms are delivered by a third-party form-to-email service (FormSubmit). Submissions pass through that service to our private inbox.

Cookies & advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and/or other sites. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Ads load only after you choose “Accept all”.

Third-party content

Price data (gold-api.com, open.er-api.com), charts (TradingView), fonts (Google Fonts) and videos (YouTube, privacy-enhanced mode) are loaded from third parties subject to their own policies.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, or unsubscribe at any time, via our contact form. We retain submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16 and contests are for adults 18+.
