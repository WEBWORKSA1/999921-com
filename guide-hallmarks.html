Chinese & Hong Kong marks

  • 足金 — ≥ 990‰; 足金999, 足金9999 for higher grades.
  • 千足金 — older label for 999 (retired in the 2014 standard revision but still seen on older pieces).
  • Au750 / K金 — 18K.
  • Maker’s mark + city code, often with a testing-centre stamp.

Middle East

Gulf and Egyptian pieces usually show 875 (21K) or 916 (22K), plus an assay office symbol. Egypt uses a small animal/figure mark with the purity number.

India

BIS hallmark: BIS logo + purity grade (e.g. 22K916) + a 6-character HUID code unique to the piece.

Warning codes — not solid gold

  • GP / GEP / HGE — gold plated / electroplated
  • GF / RGP — gold filled / rolled gold plate
  • Vermeil — gold over silver
