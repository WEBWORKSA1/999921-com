About 999921

Forever gold, explained simply.

999921.com is an independent, free resource for anyone buying, selling, gifting or simply curious about gold. The name reads two ways: 9999 fine gold and 21-karat gold — the purities that dominate China and the Middle East — and, in Chinese number-speak, 久久久久爱你, “love you forever.”

What we do

  • Live, transparent gold prices for 10 purities in 20 currencies
  • Free calculators for value, scrap, jewellery and wedding budgets
  • Plain-English guides with cited data
  • Free quote matching with vetted partners
  • Community contests with real gold prizes

Editorial standards

We cite primary sources (World Gold Council, exchanges, national standards), label estimates clearly, and correct errors fast. Sponsored content is always labelled. Found a mistake? Tell us.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, partner referral fees for quote requests, affiliate links and reader donations. None of these change our price data.
