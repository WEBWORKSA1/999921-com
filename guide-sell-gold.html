How to sell gold for the best price

The 5-step method that routinely adds 10–20% to what sellers are offered.

9
999921 Editorial · 7-min read · Updated Sept 2026

1. Weigh and sort by karat

Use a 0.01 g kitchen or jewellery scale. Group pieces by hallmark — 999, 916, 875, 750, 585. Buyers who lump everything together pay the lowest karat’s rate for all of it.

2. Calculate your melt value

Melt value = grams × purity × live price per gram. Our scrap calculator does this for mixed lots. Today 10 g of 18K is worth about — in pure metal.

3. Know typical payouts

Pawn shop / cash-for-gold kiosk50–70%Instant

Online refiner (mail-in)85–97%3–10 days
Bullion dealer (bars/coins)95–99% of spot1–3 days

4. Ask the five money questions

  1. “What percentage of melt are you paying?”
  2. “Will you weigh and test in front of me?”
  3. “Which karat are you paying for each piece?”
  4. “Any fees for testing, shipping or insurance?”
  5. “Is this offer valid if gold moves today?”
