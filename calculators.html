How to use these calculators

  • Melt value is the floor — what the pure gold inside is worth at spot.
  • Sell subtracts a dealer spread (2–5% for bullion, 10–30% for jewellery).
  • Buy adds premium, making charges and tax — typical retail for 22K/21K jewellery is 8–25% above melt.
  • Always ask a buyer to weigh in front of you and name the purity they’re paying for.
