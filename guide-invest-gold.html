Gold investing for beginners

Five ways to own gold — and what each really costs.

Context: Global gold demand topped 5,000 t for the first time in 2025 (worth US$555 bn); central banks bought 863 t; gold set 53 all-time highs and passed US$5,600/oz in January 2026 before its sharpest quarterly fall in a decade in Q2 2026. Volatility cuts both ways.

Simple rules

  1. Buy the largest bar you can afford for the lowest premium per gram.
  2. Stick to recognised refiners (LBMA / SGE-approved) for easy resale.
  3. Dollar-cost average instead of timing peaks.
  4. Factor storage and insurance into returns.
  5. Gold pays no income — size it as a hedge, not your whole portfolio.
