The density test (best free option)

Pure gold’s density is 19.32 g/cm³; 22K ≈ 17.7–17.8, 18K ≈ 15.2–15.9 (varies by alloy). Weigh the item dry, then weigh the water it displaces: density = dry weight ÷ displaced water weight. Plated brass comes in around 8.5.

Red flags: magnetic pull, green/black skin stains, “GP”, “GF”, “HGE” or “RGP” stamps (gold plated / filled), and a density far below the karat stamped.
