Chinese wedding gold — 久久久久, in solid gold

What each piece means, who traditionally buys it, and what it costs at today’s price. 999921 = love you forever — this is where the numbers become heirlooms.

The piecesWho buys whatBudget plannerGet quotes
龙凤

Dragon & phoenix bangle 龙凤镯

The dragon (groom) and phoenix (bride) — balance, harmony and a union blessed by heaven. Usually a pair, often 9999 or 999.

四点金

Si Dian Jin — the four golds

Ring, bangle, necklace and earrings, given by the groom’s family to welcome the bride. Known as Sei Dim Gam in Cantonese.

金猪

Gold pig necklace 金猪

Wishes for fertility, abundance and a prosperous household — traditionally from the groom’s mother.

九宝

The Nine Treasures

A set of nine gold ornaments — 9 for 久, a marriage that lasts. Increasingly popular in Hong Kong.
