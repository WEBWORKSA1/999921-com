The number behind the name

999921 — 久久久久爱你

In Chinese number-speak, 9 sounds like 久 (jiǔ, “forever”), 2 like 爱 (“love”) and 1 like 你 (“you”). So 999921 reads as “love you forever and ever.”

In the gold market it’s just as meaningful: 9999 is 99.99% “four nines” fine gold — the standard of the Shanghai Gold Exchange — and 21 is 21-karat gold, the favourite of Egypt and the Gulf. Forever love, measured in forever metal.

FAQ

Quick answers

What does 9999 gold mean?
9999 (“four nines”, 足金9999) is gold that is at least 99.99% pure. It’s the purity of Shanghai Gold Exchange Au99.99 bars and many investment coins. Full guide.
How pure is 21K gold?
21 ÷ 24 = 87.5% pure, hallmarked 875. It’s the most popular jewellery karat in Egypt and across the Gulf. 21K guide.
How is the gold price per gram calculated?
Spot price per troy ounce ÷ 31.1035 = price per gram of pure gold. Multiply by purity (0.875 for 21K) and your exchange rate. Our calculator does it instantly.
Why is the shop price higher than your price?
We show the value of the pure metal. Shops add a dealer premium, making/labour charges (often 5–25% for jewellery) and local tax.
What does 999921 mean in Chinese?
9999 = 久久久久 (forever and ever) and 21 = 爱你 (love you): “love you forever.” The full meaning.
Where do your prices come from?
Spot gold from a public market data feed (gold-api.com) and FX from open.er-api.com, refreshed about every 60 seconds. If a feed is unreachable we show the last known value and label it clearly.
