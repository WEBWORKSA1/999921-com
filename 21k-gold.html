Quick answer: 21K gold = 21 parts gold out of 24 = 87.5% pure. The remaining 12.5% is usually copper and silver, giving a rich yellow colour with better scratch resistance than 24K.

Why Egypt and the Gulf prefer 21K

  • Best balance of purity and hardness for everyday bangles and chains.
  • Liquidity — it is the reference karat in Egyptian and Saudi souks, so it resells fast with low spreads.
  • Colour — deeper yellow than 18K, the look buyers associate with “real” gold.
  • Savings culture — bridal “shabka” and family savings are commonly held in 21K.

Buying & selling 21K: 5 rules

  1. Check the 875 stamp and ask for an itemised invoice (weight × rate + making).
  2. Compare the shop’s per-gram rate with the live 21K rate above.
  3. Negotiate making charges — they’re the dealer’s margin, not the gold.
  4. Keep receipts; many souks buy back at near-melt with a receipt.
