The formulas

Purity % = karat ÷ 24 × 100
Hallmark = karat ÷ 24 × 1000
Karat = hallmark ÷ 1000 × 24

Example: 875 ÷ 1000 × 24 = 21K.

Chinese purity terms

  • 足金 — “full gold”, ≥ 990‰ under GB 11887.
  • 足金999 / 足金9999 — current labels for three- and four-nines gold.
  • 千足金 — former term for 999; retired in the 2014 standard revision.
  • K金 / Au750 — 18K alloy, common in Hong Kong and mainland fashion jewellery.
