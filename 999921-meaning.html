999921 — 久久久久爱你

A number that means “love you forever” — and names the two most-traded golds on earth.

Reading 1: love, forever

Chinese internet culture turns numbers into words by sound. 9 (九 jiǔ) sounds like 久 — “long-lasting.” Four of them, 9999, reads 久久久久: “forever and ever.” 2 stands for 爱 (love), as in 520 “I love you,” and 1 for 你 (you), as in 1314. Together: 久久久久爱你 — “I’ll love you forever and ever.”

Note: 999921 is a constructed reading following the same rules as established codes like 520, 1314 and 9421 (就是爱你); it is not a fixed dictionary term.

Reading 3: nine, the imperial number

Nine is the highest single yang number — the number of the emperor. The Double Ninth Festival (重阳节, on 18 October in 2026) honours elders and longevity; legend says the Forbidden City was built with 9,999½ rooms, one half-room short of heaven’s 10,000.
