How we calculate gold prices

  1. Spot price (USD per troy ounce of pure gold) from a public market feed, refreshed about every 60 seconds.
  2. ÷ 31.1035 converts troy ounces to grams.
  3. × purity: 0.9999 for 9999, 0.9167 for 22K, 0.875 for 21K, 0.75 for 18K.
  4. × FX rate for your currency (open.er-api.com, daily).
Example: 21K per gram in AED = spot ÷ 31.1035 × 0.875 × 3.6725 = —.

These are metal values, not shop prices. Retail adds premium, making charges and tax — use our calculator to include them, or get real dealer quotes.
